Our Services

Criminal law

Criminal law addresses crimes and legal defenses for those accused of wrongdoing. It covers everything from minor offenses to serious crimes, with severe consequences for convictions.

Business law

Business Law encompasses legal regulations and rules that govern business operations. It includes areas such as contracts, employment law, intellectual property, mergers and acquisitions, and commercial transactions, ensuring businesses operate within the boundaries of the law.

Civil law

Civil Law deals with disputes between individuals or organizations, typically involving issues like contracts, property, family law, and torts (personal injury or negligence).

Animal Law

Animal Law covers legal issues related to the protection and welfare of animals.

Computer Law

Computer Law focuses on legal issues related to technology, including cybersecurity, data privacy, intellectual property, software licensing, and internet regulations.

Constitutional law

Constitutional law focuses on interpreting and enforcing the principles set out in a country’s constitution. It covers individual rights, the separation of powers, and government authority.

Extradition law

Extradition law governs the process by which one country requests the transfer of a person to face criminal charges or serve a sentence in another country.

Labour law

Labour law ensures fair treatment in the workplace, covering issues like wages, discrimination, working conditions, and wrongful termination. It protects employees' rights and outlines employer responsibilities.

Natural resources law

Natural resources law governs the use and conservation of natural assets like water, minerals, forests, and energy sources. It involves regulations on environmental protection, land use, and sustainable resource management.

Law of the Sea

The Law of the Sea regulates the rights and responsibilities of nations concerning the world's oceans. It covers areas like territorial waters, exclusive economic zones, and marine conservation.

Sports law

Sports law covers legal issues related to sports and athletes, including contracts, doping, intellectual property, and dispute resolution. It also governs the rights of players, teams, and sports organizations.

Tax law

Tax law governs how individuals and businesses are taxed, including income, property, and sales taxes. It involves regulations on tax compliance, deductions, and disputes with tax authorities
